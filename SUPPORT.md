# Stumble Support

Stumble is a walking-tour app for exploring Manhattan landmarks on foot.

If something isn't working, or a landmark is wrong, email
**stumblenyc@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you.

---

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I check in at a landmark?

Open the landmark from the map or the list and tap **Check In**. Checking in
adds the landmark to your collection and counts toward your badges. You are
meant to be standing there — that's the point of the app.

### Why does Stumble need my location?

To draw your position on the map, to work out how far you are from a landmark,
and to notify you when you're near one you haven't visited. Your location is
used entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. The app does not
use background location, so it isn't tracking you when it's closed.

The app still works without location access — the map just won't follow you and
proximity notifications won't fire.

### I'm not getting proximity notifications

Check three things:

- Notifications are allowed for Stumble in iOS Settings.
- The relevant category is switched on in Stumble's own Settings screen. They
  are individually toggled, and some are off by default.
- You are within about 100 feet of a landmark you have **not** already visited.
  Visited landmarks don't notify you again.

### A landmark's details or photograph are wrong

Please tell us — email **stumblenyc@gmail.com** with the landmark name and
what's wrong. Landmark photographs come from Wikimedia Commons and are credited
in the app; occasionally an image is a poor match for the place and we'd rather
know.

### How do I suggest a landmark?

Open **Settings → Recommend a Landmark**, fill in the details and tap **Email
Recommendation**. That opens your own email app with the message prepared, so
you can review it before sending. We read every one.

### How do badges work?

There are 27 badges in three tiers. The easy ones arrive during your first walk
or two. The medium ones reward exploring widely — every category, every era,
fifteen different neighborhoods. The hard ones are for completionists, including
finishing entire neighborhoods and, eventually, all 288 landmarks.

### Does Stumble work offline?

Partly. Your visited landmarks, badges and preferences are stored on your device
and always available. The map tiles and landmark photographs need a connection,
so they won't load offline.

### How do I reset my progress?

Stumble has no accounts, so your progress lives only on your device. Deleting
and reinstalling the app clears it. This cannot be undone and we have no copy to
restore.

### Do I need an account?

No. Stumble has no sign-up, no login and no user accounts.
